## Remaining setup before sessions (your decisions)
1. **Access method (choose one):**
   - **(a) In person:** the moderator's signed-in phone or laptop. Simplest and recommended.
   - **(b) Video call:** share the moderator's screen and give remote control. Slower, and touch isn't realistic.
   - **(c) Share link:** a Vercel share link or other bypass lets participants use their own phones, but **it bypasses protection**, so it's your call.
2. **Device prep:** before each session, open the Preview, sign in to the Demo Account, then Reset demo. Close other tabs. Turn off notifications.
3. **Consent form:** have a written consent form ready if any recording is planned (recording is opt-in).
4. **Participant IDs:** P1–P5 only. Print the observation sheets.
5. **Pilot run:** do one dry run with a non-participant to time the 9 tasks (target 30–40 minutes).

## What the moderator should watch for
| Area | Watch for |
|---|---|
| Medical vs CI | Do they say "CI pays the hospital"? Did they find the ≠ callouts? |
| Life vs CI | Confusing death payout with diagnosis payout |
| Zero out-of-pocket | Do they find the bill-split section, and do they understand deductible vs co-payment? |
| Limits and exclusions | Do they read "doesn't automatically cover"? |
| Family result | Do they understand **why two numbers** (savings counted or not)? Do they read it as "you must buy RM44,000 of cover"? |
| CI result | Do they treat the lump sum as a salary? Do they adjust the recovery period? |
| Not a quote | Any sign they think the result is a price or an offer |
| Not a recommendation | Do product cards feel like endorsements? Did they notice "in no particular order" and "not affiliated"? |
| Official info | Can they find a source link and the "Needs official confirmation" list? |
| Points | Do they think points or vouchers have real value? |

Record findings with the feedback template. **Don't change formulas or content during the test period.** Review afterwards.
